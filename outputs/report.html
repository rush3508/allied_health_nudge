Allied health utilisation is the #3 strongest predictor of acute event risk (mean |SHAP| = 0.148), behind only comorbidity count (0.493) and condition cluster (0.169). In the underlying data generation process, members who use allied health have 1.88x lower acute risk – the model has independently learned this signal from claims data alone. Coverage reduces claims: every nudge that converts is a prevented ED visit.

How this is calculated:
1. Coverage candidates = members with risk_tier ∈ {Medium, High}, sessions_remaining_total > 0, and zero_allied_health_flag = 1
2. True positives = coverage candidates × Precision@top20% (39.3%) – members genuinely at elevated acute risk
3. Conversion rate = % of true positives who book an allied health visit after nudge (adjustable – default 20%)
4. Claims savings = true positives × conversion rate × cost per avoided ED visit

Conservative estimate: direct ED cost only. Does not include downstream inpatient admissions, specialist referrals, or chronic disease management savings. Real-world savings are likely 2–4x higher when these are included.
